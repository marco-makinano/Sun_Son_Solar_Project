About solar technology

How do solar panels work?

Panels turn sunlight into direct-current (DC) electricity. An inverter then converts it into the alternating-current (AC) power that your lights and appliances use.

What is the difference between a grid-tie and a hybrid inverter?

A grid-tie inverter works with the utility grid and usually shuts off during a power outage. A hybrid inverter also works with batteries, so it can keep your power on when the grid is down.

Do solar panels work on cloudy days or at night?

On cloudy days panels still make some power, just less than in full sun. At night they make none, which is why batteries are useful: they store daytime energy for later.

How long do solar panels last?

Quality panels commonly last 25 years or more, and many come with long manufacturer warranties. Ask us about the warranty of the specific product you are considering.
